# Characterise and Formulate Growing Media

[TOC]


Determine substrate properties and design mixes meeting crop requirements.

## How to Characterise and Formulate Growing Media

STAR narrative for growing media characterisation and formulation.

1. Growing media must meet crop-specific physical, chemical, and biological requirements while transitioning away from peat-based substrates with variable alternative materials.
2. Systematically characterise raw materials, formulate target-driven substrate blends, and validate through replicated crop trials against established performance benchmarks.
3. Apply CEN/ISHS standardised methods for physical and chemical characterisation, iteratively adjust component ratios, assess pathogen risk of organic alternatives, and conduct performance trials comparing candidates against peat controls.
4. Substrate properties are quantified, peat-free formulations validated for each crop type, raw material variability managed through specification control, and pathogen risks identified and mitigated.
