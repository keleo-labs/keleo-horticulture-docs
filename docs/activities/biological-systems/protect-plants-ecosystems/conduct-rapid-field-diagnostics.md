# Conduct Rapid Field Diagnostics

[TOC]


Perform point-of-care molecular or serological tests for target organisms.

## How to Conduct Rapid Field Diagnostics

STAR narrative for rapid diagnostic testing activities.

1. Suspect symptoms of regulated organisms require confirmation faster than laboratory referral can provide, with statutory notification obligations contingent on timely identification.
2. Deploy point-of-care diagnostic tests enabling on-site molecular confirmation within hours, maintaining quality assurance and chain of evidence for regulatory decision-making.
3. Select appropriate test format from LAMP, lateral flow, or portable PCR options; prepare samples following test-specific protocols; run controls; record results with full metadata; trigger escalation pathway for positive results.
4. Suspect incursions confirmed or excluded within hours, statutory notification met promptly, false positive rates managed through QA controls, and chain of evidence supports regulatory decisions.
