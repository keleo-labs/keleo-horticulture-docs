# Perform Pest and Disease Scouting

[TOC]


Systematic field surveillance to detect and quantify pest and disease incidence.

## How to Perform Pest and Disease Scouting

STAR narrative for pest and disease scouting activities.

1. Pest and disease incidence must be detected at the earliest stage and quantified against action thresholds, with both target pests and beneficial organisms assessed simultaneously.
2. Execute structured scouting programmes using manual inspection, trap monitoring, and AI-powered automated detection to generate continuous population data.
3. Follow systematic walking patterns examining leaf surfaces, stems, and root zones; deploy sticky traps and pheromone traps at canopy height; integrate AI image classification for automated continuous surveillance.
4. Pest presence detected at earliest stage, population trends tracked for threshold-based decisions, beneficial organisms quantified alongside pests, and automated monitoring extends coverage beyond manual capacity.
