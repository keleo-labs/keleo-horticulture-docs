# Implement Biosecurity Protocols

[TOC]


Enforce hygiene, quarantine, and access controls preventing pathogen introduction.

## How to Implement Biosecurity Protocols

STAR narrative for biosecurity protocol implementation.

1. Horticultural operations face continual risk of exotic pest and pathogen introduction through incoming plant material, equipment, visitors, and environmental pathways requiring layered biosecurity defences.
2. Implement, maintain, and audit physical control measures, quarantine procedures, and staff training programmes that systematically control all introduction pathways.
3. Install footbaths and tool sterilisation stations at entry points; operate quarantine spaces with independent irrigation; verify phytosanitary documentation for all incoming material; conduct periodic compliance audits.
4. Introduction pathways systematically controlled, quarantine procedures prevent undetected escape, staff competency verified and maintained, and regulatory compliance demonstrated.
