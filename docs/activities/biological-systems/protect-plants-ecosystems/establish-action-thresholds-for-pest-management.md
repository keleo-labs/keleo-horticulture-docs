# Establish Action Thresholds for Pest Management

[TOC]


Define evidence-based intervention trigger points for each pest-crop combination.

## How to Establish Action Thresholds

STAR narrative for pest management threshold establishment.

1. Intervention decisions based on calendar scheduling or reactive response lead to unnecessary chemical applications, resistance selection pressure, and inconsistent pest management outcomes.
2. Establish and calibrate evidence-based action thresholds for each pest-crop combination, integrating published economic injury levels with local field validation data.
3. Review published thresholds from extension literature, adjust for local crop values and available interventions, accumulate multi-season calibration data, integrate resistance monitoring baselines, and benchmark threshold maturity.
4. Intervention decisions based on quantified evidence, unnecessary applications eliminated, resistance risk managed proactively, and threshold maturity benchmarked against industry standards.
