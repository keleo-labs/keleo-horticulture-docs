# Design and Establish Pollinator Habitat

[TOC]


Create and manage floral resources and nesting habitat supporting pollinator populations.

## How to Design and Establish Pollinator Habitat

STAR narrative for pollinator habitat creation.

1. Pollinator populations face decline from habitat loss and agrochemical exposure, while horticultural operations depend on pollination services and could provide conservation habitat within production landscapes.
2. Design, establish, and manage permanent and annual floral resources and nesting habitat that fill temporal gaps in pollinator nutrition and support natural enemy populations.
3. Conduct baseline pollinator surveys, identify temporal and spatial resource gaps, establish native hedgerows and wildflower meadows with sequential flowering, manage habitat avoiding nesting site destruction, and monitor population responses.
4. Pollinator populations supported through continuous floral provision, habitat connectivity enhanced across the production landscape, conservation biocontrol strengthened, and BNG obligations advanced.
