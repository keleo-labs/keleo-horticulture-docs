# Manage Resistance in Pest Populations

[TOC]


Implement strategies preventing or delaying pest resistance to control measures.

## How to Manage Resistance in Pest Populations

STAR narrative for resistance management activities.

1. Pest resistance to chemical and biological control agents threatens the effective life of available interventions, with over 600 insect species showing resistance development.
2. Implement mode-of-action diversity, conduct sensitivity monitoring to detect resistance shifts before field-level control failure, and maintain label-rate application discipline.
3. Characterise resistance risk profiles for target pests; document rotation schedules mandating different mode-of-action groups; conduct baseline sensitivity assays; periodically re-test to detect dose-response shifts; report monitoring data.
4. Effective life of control products maximised, resistance detected before field failure, mode-of-action diversity maintained, and industry-level resistance intelligence contributed and utilised.
