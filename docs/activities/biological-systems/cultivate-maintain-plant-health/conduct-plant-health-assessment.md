# Conduct Plant Health Assessment

[TOC]


Systematic evaluation of plant vitality across a defined population.

## How to Conduct Plant Health Assessment

STAR narrative for performing plant health assessments.

1. Plant condition across a growing population must be quantified to detect emerging threats before they reach damaging levels and to compare cultivar performance across environments.
2. Deploy both visual and instrumental assessment methods to generate structured health records enabling trend analysis and evidence-based management decisions.
3. Conduct systematic sampling using phenotyping tools including multispectral imaging, chlorophyll fluorescence, and canopy reflectance analysis, recording data against standardised severity scales with statistical rigour.
4. Plant condition is quantified and documented, emerging threats are detected early, performance differences between cultivars are identified, and management interventions are triggered by evidence.
