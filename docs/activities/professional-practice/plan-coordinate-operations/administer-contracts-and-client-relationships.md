# Administer Contracts and Client Relationships

[TOC]


Management of client engagement from initial enquiry through contract delivery to aftercare.

## Contract Administration Technique

Technique for structured client engagement aligned with professional standards.

1. UK households spent 5.8 billion GBP on professional horticultural services in 2023, requiring structured client engagement aligned with Landscape Institute and BALI professional standards.
2. Establish contract administration processes covering scoping, specification development, pricing, delivery management, and aftercare.
3. Implement structured site assessment, technical specification writing using botanical nomenclature, pricing from job costing data, and progress reporting aligned with BALI Code of Conduct requirements.
4. Standardized client engagement delivering consistent quality, managing horticultural-specific risks, and measuring satisfaction through structured feedback.
