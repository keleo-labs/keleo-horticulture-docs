# Conduct Financial Planning and Performance Analysis

[TOC]


Preparation of financial forecasts, budgets, and performance analysis for business management.

## Financial Planning Technique

Technique for financial forecasting and performance analysis in seasonal horticulture.

1. Horticultural enterprises face seasonal revenue peaks in spring and summer, weather-dependent productivity, and the combination of upfront material costs with delayed client payment.
2. Prepare annual budgets, monthly cash flow forecasts, and financial performance reports enabling evidence-based management decisions.
3. Model productive hours after training and weather downtime, calculate labour costs against the supervisory ratio, prepare cash flow forecasts, and benchmark against Farm Business Survey data.
4. Monthly financial reporting with variance analysis, sector benchmarking, and cash flow management enabling business resilience through seasonal troughs.
