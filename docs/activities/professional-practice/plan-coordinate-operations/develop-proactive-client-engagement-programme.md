# Develop Proactive Client Engagement Programme

[TOC]


Establishment of ongoing client value development through advisory services and relationship nurturing.

## Proactive Client Engagement Technique

Technique for extending client relationships beyond initial delivery.

1. After initial project delivery, many horticultural client relationships lapse without structured follow-up, leaving maintenance, advisory, and seasonal services unexplored.
2. Develop proactive engagement programmes that extend client relationships beyond initial delivery through advisory communications, maintenance proposals, and community engagement.
3. Implement seasonal advisory communications, propose maintenance programmes post-completion, track referral and repeat business rates, incorporate feedback into service improvement, and conduct public engagement activities where appropriate.
4. Increased client lifetime value through ongoing maintenance relationships, measurable referral rates, and community engagement contributing to business reputation and social impact.
