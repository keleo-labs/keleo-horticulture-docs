# Conduct Strategic Business Development

[TOC]


Identification and pursuit of growth opportunities aligned with sector trends.

## Strategic Business Development Technique

Technique for identifying and pursuing sector growth opportunities.

1. The sector projects growth from 38 billion to 51.2 billion GBP by 2030, with emerging opportunities in biodiversity net gain, green infrastructure, and sustainable urban drainage systems.
2. Analyse emerging market opportunities, develop strategic partnerships, and position the business for growth aligned with policy trends and technology adoption.
3. Evaluate biodiversity net gain demand, assess peat-free expertise as competitive advantage, engage with industry bodies, respond to policy consultations, and pursue technology grants recommended by the House of Lords Committee.
4. Strategic diversification into growth markets, funded technology pathways, and industry leadership positioning through policy engagement and professional body participation.
