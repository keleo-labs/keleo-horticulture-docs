# Embed Organisational Learning Culture

[TOC]


Integration of knowledge sharing, cross-functional development, and continuous learning into daily operations.

## Organisational Learning Culture Technique

Technique for transforming informal knowledge sharing into structured learning culture.

1. Horticultural knowledge has traditionally been transmitted informally through on-the-job learning, limiting scalability and creating risk when experienced practitioners retire.
2. Transform informal knowledge sharing into a structured learning culture with peer learning, cross-functional development, and continuous assessment aligned with Skills England reforms.
3. Establish structured toolbox talks and technical demonstrations, host apprentices and student placements, develop cross-functional skills enabling workforce flexibility, and adopt continuous workplace assessment methods.
4. The organization is recognized as a quality training provider with embedded knowledge-sharing practices and cross-functional capability enabling operational resilience.
