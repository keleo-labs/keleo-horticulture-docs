# Assess Workforce Capability Baseline

[TOC]


Systematic audit of current workforce qualifications, skills, and demographic profile against requirements.

## Workforce Capability Assessment Technique

Technique for auditing workforce qualifications and benchmarking against sector data.

1. The horticultural workforce is characterised by informal skill acquisition, an average decision-maker age of 55, and skill gaps that are poorly documented at business level.
2. Conduct a comprehensive audit of current workforce qualifications, certifications, and competency levels benchmarked against AHDB Skills Survey findings and business requirements.
3. Document individual qualifications and certification status, benchmark the workforce profile against sector data including the 3% supervisory ratio, identify succession risks and demographic vulnerabilities, and map gaps to available qualification pathways.
4. A documented capability baseline identifying specific skill gaps, succession risks, and development priorities enabling structured workforce investment decisions.
