# Develop Business Plan

[TOC]


Creation and maintenance of a strategic business plan for a horticultural enterprise.

## Business Plan Development Technique

Technique for developing a business plan using authoritative sector data.

1. A horticultural enterprise requires a documented business strategy covering market positioning, financial projections, and workforce planning in a sector contributing 38 billion GBP to UK GDP.
2. Develop a business plan using authoritative sector data from Oxford Economics and Defra that accounts for seasonal revenue patterns and the industry's supervisory capacity gap.
3. Conduct market analysis, prepare three-year financial forecasts, model labour costs against the AHDB workforce findings, and appraise capital investment with cost-benefit analysis.
4. A structured business plan guiding investment decisions, workforce strategy, and market positioning aligned with the sector's growth trajectory.
