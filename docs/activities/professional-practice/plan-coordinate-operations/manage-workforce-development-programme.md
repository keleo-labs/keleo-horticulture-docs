# Manage Workforce Development Programme

[TOC]


Coordination of structured training, qualification attainment, and career progression.

## Workforce Development Programme Technique

Technique for formalizing workforce development through qualification pathways.

1. The horticultural workforce has an ageing demographic with an average key decision-maker age of 55, predominantly informal skill acquisition, and a critical 3% supervisory ratio constraining growth.
2. Formalize workforce development through documented pathways aligned with Skills England apprenticeship standards, RHS qualifications, and CIH CPD requirements.
3. Establish individual development plans, apprenticeship programmes targeting ST0225 and ST0226 standards, supervisory development initiatives, and CPD tracking systems meeting professional body requirements.
4. Measurable improvement in qualification attainment rates, internal promotions from operative to supervisory roles, and workforce retention through structured development investment.
