# Establish Regulatory Compliance Framework

[TOC]


Identification and systematic organisation of all regulatory obligations applicable to the enterprise.

## Regulatory Framework Establishment Technique

Technique for auditing and organizing regulatory obligations.

1. Horticultural operations face multi-layered regulatory requirements spanning pesticide use, plant health, environmental permitting, and equipment safety with no grandfather rights for pesticide certification.
2. Audit all applicable legislation and organize obligations into a structured compliance register with responsible persons and review schedules.
3. Map requirements across HSE, COSHH, BTOM phytosanitary categories, T23 waste exemptions, and professional body standards into a comprehensive register with evidence tracking.
4. A complete compliance framework with assigned responsibilities, documented evidence, and review schedules enabling consistent regulatory adherence.
