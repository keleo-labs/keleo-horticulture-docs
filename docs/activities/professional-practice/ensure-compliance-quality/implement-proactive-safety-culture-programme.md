# Implement Proactive Safety Culture Programme

[TOC]


Development of leading safety indicators, behavioural observations, and wellbeing support initiatives.

## Proactive Safety Culture Technique

Technique for building safety culture with leading indicators and wellbeing support.

1. Many horticultural businesses manage safety reactively through incident response and compliance checking, without tracking leading indicators or addressing the mental health challenges of physically demanding outdoor work.
2. Build a proactive safety culture that prevents incidents through behavioural observation, leading indicator tracking, worker engagement, and wellbeing support.
3. Implement behavioural safety observation programmes, track leading indicators including training completion and near-miss reporting rates, establish safety committees with worker consultation, provide mental health support, and pursue ISO 45001 accreditation.
4. Safety performance exceeding sector benchmarks, zero serious harm track record, ISO 45001 certification, and safety practices referenced as industry benchmarks.
