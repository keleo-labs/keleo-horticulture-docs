# Manage Pesticide Compliance

[TOC]


Administration of all regulatory requirements for professional pesticide purchase, storage, and application.

## Pesticide Compliance Management Technique

Technique for maintaining pesticide certification and COSHH compliance.

1. Since November 2015, no grandfather rights exist for pesticide use; all professional users must hold a Specified Certificate including PA1 theory and PA6 practical assessment.
2. Maintain a register of all certificate holders, ensure COSHH compliance for every product, and manage compliant storage and application records.
3. Track certificate validity, complete COSHH assessments for all products, maintain secure bunded storage, record application details, and obtain specialist units such as PA6AW for near-water application.
4. Full pesticide compliance with auditable records, compliant storage, and a workforce certified to the appropriate specialist level.
