# Manage Quality Assurance and Standards Compliance

[TOC]


Systematic assurance that service delivery meets quality standards and certification requirements.

## Quality Assurance Technique

Technique for systematic quality standards and continuous audit readiness.

1. BALI Accredited Contractor assessment evaluates completed work quality on-site; GLOBALG.A.P. IFA v6 introduced unannounced inspections requiring continuous compliance rather than audit-preparation approaches.
2. Establish quality standards for each service line and implement monitoring combining self-inspection, supervisory checking, and independent assessment.
3. Define workmanship standards referencing British Standards, implement quality inspection schedules, collect and analyse client feedback systematically, and maintain continuous audit readiness for certification schemes.
4. Consistent service quality meeting BALI assessment standards, continuous GLOBALG.A.P. compliance, and quality-driven client satisfaction improving referral and retention rates.
