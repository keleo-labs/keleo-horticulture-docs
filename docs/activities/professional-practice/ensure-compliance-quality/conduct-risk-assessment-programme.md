# Conduct Risk Assessment Programme

[TOC]


Systematic assessment of hazards across all operational activities with documented controls.

## Risk Assessment Technique

Technique for systematic hazard assessment with COSHH compliance.

1. Horticultural operations present distinctive hazards including outdoor exposure, powered machinery, COSHH-regulated chemicals, and working at height requiring systematic risk assessment.
2. Complete risk assessments following the hierarchy of control for all activities, with particular emphasis on COSHH and high-risk operations.
3. Identify hazards, complete COSHH assessments for every chemical product, develop site-specific assessments, and create method statements for high-risk activities communicated through toolbox talks.
4. A comprehensive risk assessment portfolio with COSHH compliance, site-specific controls, and safe systems of work for all operations.
