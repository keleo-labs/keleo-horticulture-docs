# Manage Phytosanitary and Plant Health Compliance

[TOC]


Administration of plant health regulations, import controls, and biosecurity requirements.

## Phytosanitary Compliance Technique

Technique for managing post-Brexit plant health requirements.

1. Post-Brexit phytosanitary requirements under the Border Target Operating Model classify plant imports by risk level, with phytosanitary certificates valid for 13 days and physical inspections required at Border Control Posts.
2. Classify all plant material sources against BTOM categories, establish IPAFFS registration, and manage Northern Ireland regulatory divergence under the Windsor Framework.
3. Map sourcing to risk categories, coordinate phytosanitary certification timing, establish Border Control Post logistics, update procedures for the 2025 Phytosanitary Conditions Amendment, and monitor regulatory evolution.
4. Compliant plant procurement with BTOM-categorized sourcing, operational IPAFFS procedures, and proactive monitoring of the annually evolving phytosanitary landscape.

> See: [Defra Plant Health and Import Controls 2024-2025](../../../references.md#defra-plant-health-and-import-controls-2024-2025)
