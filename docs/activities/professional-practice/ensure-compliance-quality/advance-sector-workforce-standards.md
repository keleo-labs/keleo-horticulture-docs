# Advance Sector Workforce Standards

[TOC]


Contribution to developing apprenticeship standards, qualification frameworks, and professional competency models.

## Sector Workforce Standards Advancement Technique

Technique for contributing to workforce standard development through partnership and publication.

1. Sector workforce standards are developed through collaboration between employers, awarding bodies, and professional institutions, with limited participation from smaller horticultural businesses.
2. Position the organization as a contributor to workforce standard development through partnership with educational institutions and professional bodies.
3. Participate in apprenticeship standard revision, contribute to qualification framework development, publish workforce case studies, partner with colleges in curriculum design, and serve on professional body competency committees.
4. The organization's workforce development model is referenced by industry bodies as exemplary, with demonstrable contribution to sector workforce standards and outcomes exceeding published benchmarks.
