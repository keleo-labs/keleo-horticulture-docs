# Maintain Professional Certifications and Industry Accreditation

[TOC]


Ongoing management of professional body memberships, quality scheme certifications, and CPD compliance.

## Professional Certification Management Technique

Technique for maintaining certifications and CPD across multiple schemes.

1. Multiple professional frameworks require ongoing management: BALI requires on-site assessment; CIH Chartered membership requires 30 hours CPD annually across six categories; Arboricultural Association Fellows require 20 hours.
2. Maintain all certifications in good standing, track CPD compliance, and plan pathways to higher professional recognition including Chartered status.
3. Centralize certification tracking, cross-reference evidence across schemes to reduce duplication, maintain CPD portfolios, and coordinate renewal workflows across multiple certification bodies.
4. All individual and business certifications current, CPD programmes exceeding professional body requirements, and structured pathways to Chartered or Fellow status.
