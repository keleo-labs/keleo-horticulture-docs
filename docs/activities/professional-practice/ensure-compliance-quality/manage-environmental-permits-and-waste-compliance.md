# Manage Environmental Permits and Waste Compliance

[TOC]


Administration of environmental permits, waste exemptions, and ecological compliance obligations.

## Environmental Permits Management Technique

Technique for managing waste exemptions, permits, and peat reduction.

1. The tiered waste management framework allows T23 composting up to 80 tonnes, with Standard Rules permits required for larger operations; invasive species contaminated materials create controlled waste obligations.
2. Assess all waste streams, obtain required exemptions and permits, and manage peat reduction and invasive species disposal obligations.
3. Register T23 exemptions or obtain permits, implement duty of care documentation, establish invasive species disposal procedures, track peat reduction against GLOBALG.A.P. targets, and monitor the Environment Agency composting framework review.
4. Compliant waste management across all streams, PAS 100 certification where applicable, and measurable progress toward peat elimination.
