# Lead Regulatory and Standards Development

[TOC]


Active contribution to drafting legislation, standards committees, and regulatory policy advocacy.

## Regulatory Leadership Technique

Technique for influencing regulatory development through committee membership and evidence submissions.

1. The horticultural sector's regulatory landscape evolves through government consultations, standards body revisions, and industry working groups, with limited practitioner input at the policy level.
2. Position the organization to directly influence regulatory development through committee membership, evidence submissions, and published research informing policy decisions.
3. Join standards development committees, submit evidence to government consultations, publish compliance case studies, develop cross-sector regulatory relationships, and shape future frameworks through thought leadership.
4. Direct contribution to regulatory evolution with the organization's practices cited as benchmarks by inspection bodies and certification schemes.
