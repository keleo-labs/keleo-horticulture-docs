# Execute Preventive Infrastructure Maintenance

[TOC]


Conduct scheduled and condition-based maintenance preventing equipment degradation and failure.

## Execute Preventive Infrastructure Maintenance Technique

Technique guidance for systematic preventive maintenance execution.

1. Installed technical infrastructure requires systematic preventive maintenance to prevent failure, extend equipment lifespan, and maintain operational reliability.
2. Execute documented maintenance procedures on schedule, track component wear, and maintain spare parts inventory for critical systems.
3. Follow manufacturer-specified maintenance schedules for HVAC, sensors, pumps, and mechanical components, log all maintenance actions with parts used and condition observations, and maintain spare parts inventory at levels preventing extended downtime.
4. Equipment reliability maintained with documented maintenance history, extended component lifespan, and minimized unplanned downtime through systematic preventive care.
