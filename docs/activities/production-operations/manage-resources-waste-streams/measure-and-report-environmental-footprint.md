# Measure and Report Environmental Footprint

[TOC]


Quantify greenhouse gas emissions, water use, and resource consumption.

## How to Measure and Report Environmental Footprint

STAR narrative for environmental footprint measurement.

1. Environmental impact of horticultural operations must be quantified using standardised methods to identify emission hotspots, track reduction progress, and meet certification requirements.
2. Conduct systematic environmental footprint measurement following PAS 2050-1 or equivalent methodology, covering GHG emissions, water use, waste generation, and energy consumption.
3. Define system boundaries and impact categories; collect activity data for emission sources; apply emission factors from recognised databases; compile results into standardised reporting formats; identify hotspots for targeted reduction.
4. Environmental impact quantified, emission hotspots identified, reduction progress tracked against baseline, peer benchmarking enabled, and reporting meets certification and regulatory requirements.
