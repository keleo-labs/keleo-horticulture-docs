# Implement Water and Nutrient Conservation

[TOC]


Deploy strategies reducing water and nutrient waste while maintaining productivity.

## Implement Water and Nutrient Conservation Technique

Technique guidance for deploying water and nutrient conservation strategies.

1. Conventional irrigation and nutrient management generates waste through over-application, runoff, and excess energy consumption.
2. Implement conservation strategies achieving documented reduction in water, nutrient, and energy consumption.
3. Deploy regulated deficit irrigation, install LED lighting replacing HPS, configure adaptive lighting controls, implement nutrient recirculation, and install thermal conservation measures.
4. Documented resource consumption reduction with LED replacement achieving 24% electricity savings and RDI strategies improving water use efficiency by 40-56%.
