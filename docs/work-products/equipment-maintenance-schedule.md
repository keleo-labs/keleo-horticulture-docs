# Equipment Maintenance Schedule

[TOC]


Time-based and condition-based maintenance planning for all infrastructure.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Calendar Schedule

Fixed-interval maintenance tasks by equipment category and date.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Tasks listed with dates**: Maintenance tasks listed by equipment category with scheduled dates.
- [ ] **Personnel assigned**: Task descriptions and responsible personnel assigned.
- [ ] **Frequency aligned with manufacturer**: Scheduling frequency aligned with manufacturer recommendations.

**Contributes to:**

- [Technical Infrastructure](../concerns/production-operations/technical-infrastructure.md) — [Maintained](../concerns/production-operations/technical-infrastructure.md#3-maintained)

**Developed by:**

- [Construct and Commission Growing Environment](../activities/production-operations/deploy-maintain-technical-infrastructure/construct-and-commission-growing-environment.md)

### 2. Detailed Maintenance Plan

Calendar schedule enhanced with procedures and safety precautions.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Procedures documented per type**: Manufacturer-specified procedures documented per equipment type.
- [ ] **Parts and duration recorded**: Required parts, consumables, and estimated duration recorded.
- [ ] **Safety precautions specified**: Safety precautions and compliance requirements specified.

**Contributes to:**

- [Technical Infrastructure](../concerns/production-operations/technical-infrastructure.md) — [Maintained](../concerns/production-operations/technical-infrastructure.md#3-maintained)

**Developed by:**

- [Execute Preventive Infrastructure Maintenance](../activities/production-operations/deploy-maintain-technical-infrastructure/execute-preventive-infrastructure-maintenance.md)

### 3. Condition-Based Maintenance Plan

Detailed plan augmented with IoT monitoring and predictive thresholds.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **IoT monitoring parameters specified**: IoT condition monitoring parameters specified per equipment class.
- [ ] **Degradation thresholds defined**: Degradation thresholds triggering maintenance intervention defined.
- [ ] **Transition to condition-based documented**: Documented transition from time-based to condition-based scheduling recorded.

**Contributes to:**
