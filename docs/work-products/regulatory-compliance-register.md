# Regulatory Compliance Register

[TOC]


Consolidated record of applicable regulations, compliance status, and review schedules.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Checklist

Basic compliance status indicators for each obligation.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Regulatory Obligations Listed**: All applicable regulatory obligations are listed with status indicators.
- [ ] **Core Requirements Included**: Pesticide certification, COSHH, and insurance requirements are included.
- [ ] **Status Recording**: Basic compliant/non-compliant status for each item is recorded.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Aware](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#1-aware)

### 2. Detailed Register

Comprehensive register with responsible persons and dates.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Responsible Persons Assigned**: Responsible persons assigned to each regulatory obligation.
- [ ] **Evidence Documentation**: Evidence documentation referenced and review dates recorded.
- [ ] **Forthcoming Changes Captured**: Forthcoming regulatory changes captured with impact assessment.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Compliant](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#2-compliant)

**Developed by:**

- [Establish Regulatory Compliance Framework](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/establish-regulatory-compliance-framework.md)
- [Manage Pesticide Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-pesticide-compliance.md)
- [Manage Environmental Permits and Waste Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-environmental-permits-and-waste-compliance.md)
- [Manage Phytosanitary and Plant Health Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-phytosanitary-and-plant-health-compliance.md)

### 3. Integrated System

Dynamic compliance management with automated alerts.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Automated Renewal Alerts**: Automated renewal alerts for time-limited certifications are configured.
- [ ] **Audit Trail**: Audit trail recording all compliance activities is maintained.
- [ ] **Dashboard Reporting**: Dashboard reporting of compliance status across the organization is operational.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Proactive](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#4-proactive)

**Developed by:**

- [Manage Quality Assurance and Standards Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-quality-assurance-and-standards-compliance.md)
