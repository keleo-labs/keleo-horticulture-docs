# <i class="fa-solid fa-virus-slash" style="font-size:20px"></i> Biosecurity Risk Assessment

[TOC]


Evaluation of pathogen and pest introduction pathways and mitigations.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Pathway Checklist

Enumeration of introduction pathways with basic mitigations.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Introduction pathways enumerated**: Introduction pathways enumerated including imports, visitors, and water sources by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Basic mitigations listed**: Basic mitigation measures listed for each identified pathway by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Dispersal routes assessed**: Wind-borne and vector-mediated dispersal routes assessed, using established criteria and documented methodology with results validated by qualified practitioners.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Surveilled](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#1-surveilled)

### 2. Scored Risk Matrix

Systematic assessment scoring pathways by likelihood and consequence.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Pathways scored by likelihood and**: Each pathway scored by likelihood and consequence using structured matrix by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **High-priority risks identified**: Plant health metrics, diagnostic findings, and intervention recommendations documented using standardized assessment protocols with photographic evidence and laboratory confirmations where applicable.
- [ ] **Import categorisation framework applied**: Risk-based import categorisation framework applied per Defra guidance by internal or external auditors.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Prevented](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#2-prevented)

**Developed by:**

- [Implement Biosecurity Protocols](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/implement-biosecurity-protocols.md)

### 3. Integrated Biosecurity Plan

Comprehensive risk management with surveillance and contingency planning.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Surveillance and quarantine**: Plant health metrics, diagnostic findings, and intervention recommendations documented using standardized assessment protocols with photographic evidence and laboratory confirmations where applicable.
- [ ] **Diagnostic capability mapped**: Diagnostic capability mapping completed with contingency response plans by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Training and audit aligned**: Staff training records and annual audit schedules aligned with Plant Healthy scheme by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Contingency plans address high-priority organisms**: Contingency plans address each high-priority organism identified in risk matrix by internal or external auditors.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Contained](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#4-contained)
- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Resilient](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#6-resilient)

**Developed by:**
