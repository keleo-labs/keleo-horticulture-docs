# <i class="fa-solid fa-virus-slash" style="font-size:20px"></i> Biosecurity Risk Assessment

[TOC]


Evaluation of pathogen and pest introduction pathways and mitigations.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Pathway Checklist

Enumeration of introduction pathways with basic mitigations.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Introduction pathways enumerated: Introduction pathways enumerated including imports, visitors, and water sources.
- [ ] Basic mitigations listed: Basic mitigation measures listed for each identified pathway.
- [ ] Dispersal routes assessed: Wind-borne and vector-mediated dispersal routes assessed.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Surveilled](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#1-surveilled)

### 2. Scored Risk Matrix

Systematic assessment scoring pathways by likelihood and consequence.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Pathways scored by likelihood and consequence: Each pathway scored by likelihood and consequence using structured matrix.
- [ ] High-priority risks identified: High-priority risks identified with specific control measures documented.
- [ ] Import categorisation framework applied: Risk-based import categorisation framework applied per Defra guidance.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Prevented](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#2-prevented)

**Developed by:**

- [Implement Biosecurity Protocols](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/implement-biosecurity-protocols.md)

### 3. Integrated Biosecurity Plan

Comprehensive risk management with surveillance and contingency planning.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Surveillance and quarantine documented: Surveillance protocols and quarantine procedures documented.
- [ ] Diagnostic capability mapped: Diagnostic capability mapping completed with contingency response plans.
- [ ] Training and audit aligned: Staff training records and annual audit schedules aligned with Plant Healthy scheme.
- [ ] Contingency plans address high-priority organisms: Contingency plans address each high-priority organism identified in risk matrix.

**Contributes to:**

- [Biosecurity](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md) — [Contained](../concerns/biological-systems/biosecurity-ecosystem-health.md#4-contained)
