# Workforce Development Plan

[TOC]


Structured programme linking individual capability to business requirements and qualifications.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Training Record

Basic record of qualifications per employee.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Current Qualifications Logged: Current qualifications and certifications for each team member are logged.
- [ ] Expiry Date Tracking: Expiry dates for time-limited certificates are tracked.
- [ ] Mandatory Training Status: Mandatory training completion status is recorded.

**Contributes to:**

- [Workforce Development](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md) — [Identified](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md#1-identified)

**Developed by:**

- [Assess Workforce Capability Baseline](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/assess-workforce-capability-baseline.md)

### 2. Development Plan

Individual plans mapping employees to pathways.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Target Qualifications Documented: Target qualifications and competency development goals are documented per employee.
- [ ] Apprenticeship Milestones Tracked: Apprenticeship milestones and CPD activities are tracked with timelines.
- [ ] Training Budget Aggregated: Business-level training budget and schedule are aggregated.

**Contributes to:**

- [Workforce Development](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md) — [Structured](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md#2-structured)

**Developed by:**

- [Manage Workforce Development Programme](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/manage-workforce-development-programme.md)
- [Maintain Professional Certifications and Industry Accreditation](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/maintain-professional-certifications-and-industry-accreditation.md)

### 3. Strategic Workforce Plan

Multi-year capability strategy with succession planning.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Recruitment Pipeline Design: Recruitment pipelines and apprenticeship programme design are documented.
- [ ] Supervisory Development Pathways: Supervisory development pathways targeting the 3% gap are addressed.
- [ ] Training ROI Analysis: Return-on-investment analysis for training expenditure is included.

**Contributes to:**
