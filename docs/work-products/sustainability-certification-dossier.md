# <i class="fa-solid fa-certificate" style="font-size:20px"></i> Sustainability Certification Dossier

[TOC]


Compiled evidence demonstrating compliance with environmental certification standards.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Self-Assessment Record

Completed self-assessment identifying compliance gaps.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Certification requirements assessed: Requirements of target certification scheme assessed point by point.
- [ ] Compliance gaps identified: Areas of compliance and gaps requiring remediation identified.
- [ ] Remediation timeline established: Remediation timeline established for identified gaps.

**Contributes to:**

- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Measured](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#1-measured)

**Developed by:**

- [Measure and Report Environmental Footprint](../activities/production-operations/manage-resources-waste-streams/measure-and-report-environmental-footprint.md)

### 2. Audit-Ready Evidence Pack

Structured portfolio prepared for external auditor review.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Evidence organised by requirement: Policies, procedures, and monitoring records organised by certification requirement.
- [ ] Improvement plans documented: Improvement plans documented with measurable targets.
- [ ] Evidence portfolio prepared: Evidence portfolio prepared for external auditor examination.

**Contributes to:**

- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Reduced](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#2-reduced)

**Developed by:**

- [Implement Peat-Free Growing Media Transition](../activities/biological-systems/establish-manage-growing-environments/implement-peat-free-growing-media-transition.md)

Dossier demonstrating simultaneous multi-standard compliance.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Multiple standards cross-referenced: LEAF Marque, MPS-GAP, GLOBALG.A.P., and BNG requirements cross-referenced.
- [ ] Overlapping requirements mapped: Overlapping requirements mapped to eliminate evidence duplication.
- [ ] Continuous audit readiness maintained: Continuous audit readiness maintained across all certified standards.

**Contributes to:**

- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Systemic](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#6-systemic)
