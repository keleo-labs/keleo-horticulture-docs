# <i class="fa-solid fa-flask" style="font-size:20px"></i> Growing Media Specification

[TOC]


Formulation and performance requirements for plant growing substrates.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Component List

Substrate components, proportions, and target application.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Components named with proportions**: All substrate components named with proportions by volume specified by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Target application identified**: Target crop or growing application identified for the formulation by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Source sustainability**: Detailed technical requirements, material sources, mixing ratios, and performance criteria specified with validation testing results and supplier certifications included.

**Contributes to:**

- [Growing Environment](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md) — [Characterized](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md#1-characterized)

**Developed by:**

- [Characterise and Formulate Growing Media](../activities/biological-systems/establish-manage-growing-environments/characterise-and-formulate-growing-media.md)

### 2. Physical and Chemical Profile

Full characterisation with tolerance ranges per parameter.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Physical properties quantified**: Air-filled porosity, water-holding capacity, and bulk density quantified by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Chemical properties determined**: pH, EC, cation exchange capacity, and baseline nutrients determined by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Tolerance ranges**: Detailed technical requirements, material sources, mixing ratios, and performance criteria specified with validation testing results and supplier certifications included.
- [ ] **Standardised methods referenced**: CEN or ISHS standardised methods referenced for all measurements by internal or external auditors.

**Contributes to:**

- [Growing Environment](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md) — [Prepared](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md#2-prepared)

**Developed by:**

- [Characterise and Formulate Growing Media](../activities/biological-systems/establish-manage-growing-environments/characterise-and-formulate-growing-media.md)

### 3. Performance-Validated Formulation

Specification with documented crop trial results.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Crop trials demonstrate performance**: Replicated crop trials demonstrate performance meeting defined benchmarks by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Peat-free transition evidence provided**: Detailed technical requirements, material sources, mixing ratios, and performance criteria specified with validation testing results and supplier certifications included.
- [ ] **Pathogen risk assessed**: Pathogen risk assessment completed for organic alternative components, using established criteria and documented methodology with results validated by qualified practitioners.
- [ ] **Substrate stability validated**: Substrate stability over the production cycle validated by internal or external auditors.

**Contributes to:**

- [Growing Environment](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md) — [Balanced](../concerns/biological-systems/growing-environment.md#3-balanced)
- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Reduced](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#2-reduced)

**Developed by:**

- [Characterise and Formulate Growing Media](../activities/biological-systems/establish-manage-growing-environments/characterise-and-formulate-growing-media.md)
- [Implement Peat-Free Growing Media Transition](../activities/biological-systems/establish-manage-growing-environments/implement-peat-free-growing-media-transition.md)

### 4. Lifecycle-Managed Substrate Standard

Comprehensive specification with circular economy credentials.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Sourcing sustainability incorporated**: Sourcing sustainability and carbon footprint data incorporated by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **End-of-life pathway**: Detailed technical requirements, material sources, mixing ratios, and performance criteria specified with validation testing results and supplier certifications included.
- [ ] **Circular economy credentials verified**: Circular economy credentials verified for the complete material cycle with documented evidence and cross-referenced against RHS standards or equivalent professional benchmarks.
- [ ] **Specification version-controlled**: Detailed technical requirements, material sources, mixing ratios, and performance criteria specified with validation testing results and supplier certifications included.

**Contributes to:**

**Developed by:**
