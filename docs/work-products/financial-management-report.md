# Financial Management Report

[TOC]


Periodic financial analysis covering profitability, cash flow, and budget performance.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Basic Accounts

Profit and loss summary with cash position.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Monthly Income Summary**: Monthly income, expenditure, and profit summary is prepared.
- [ ] **Cash Position Statement**: Cash position with outstanding debtors and creditors is shown.
- [ ] **Tax Payment Tracking**: Tax payment obligations are tracked.

**Contributes to:**

- [Financial Management](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md) — [Recorded](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md#1-recorded)

**Developed by:**

- [Develop Business Plan](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/develop-business-plan.md)

### 2. Management Accounts

Detailed reporting with variance analysis.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Service Line Profitability**: Profitability broken down by service line or client category.
- [ ] **Variance and Labour Analysis**: Budget variance analysis and labour cost analysis are included.
- [ ] **Quarterly Cash Flow Forecast**: Cash flow forecasting for the upcoming quarter is prepared.

**Contributes to:**

- [Financial Management](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md) — [Analyzed](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md#3-analyzed)
- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Monitored](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#3-monitored)

**Developed by:**

- [Administer Contracts and Client Relationships](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/administer-contracts-and-client-relationships.md)
- [Conduct Financial Planning and Performance Analysis](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/conduct-financial-planning-and-performance-analysis.md)
- [Develop Proactive Client Engagement Programme](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/develop-proactive-client-engagement-programme.md)

### 3. Strategic Financial Report

Comprehensive analysis with benchmarking.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Multi-Period Trend Analysis**: Multi-period trend analysis and sector benchmarking are incorporated.
- [ ] **Investment Return Analysis**: Capital investment return analysis and scenario modelling are included.
- [ ] **KPI Dashboards**: KPI dashboards for management decision-making are provided.

**Contributes to:**

- [Financial Management](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md) — [Strategic](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md#5-strategic)
- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Strategic](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#5-strategic)

**Developed by:**
