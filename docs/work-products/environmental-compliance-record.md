# Environmental Compliance Record

[TOC]


Documented evidence of environmental regulatory compliance including permits and impact assessments.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Basic Records

Waste transfer notes, exemptions, and records.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Registered Waste Exemptions**: Registered waste exemptions such as T23 are documented.
- [ ] **Waste Transfer Notes Filed**: Waste transfer notes for all waste movements are filed.
- [ ] **Environmental Incident Records**: Environmental incident records are maintained.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Compliant](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#2-compliant)

### 2. Managed System

Comprehensive records with monitoring and improvement.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Waste and Peat Reduction Tracking**: Waste minimization data and peat reduction progress are tracked.
- [ ] **Resource Consumption Monitoring**: Water usage and energy consumption monitoring are operational.
- [ ] **Incident Logs with Corrective Actions**: Environmental incident logs with corrective actions are maintained.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Certified](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#3-certified)

**Developed by:**

- [Manage Environmental Permits and Waste Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-environmental-permits-and-waste-compliance.md)
- [Manage Phytosanitary and Plant Health Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-phytosanitary-and-plant-health-compliance.md)

### 3. Auditable System

Environmental management system meeting ISO 14001.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Environmental Policy and Controls**: Environmental policy, objectives, targets, and operational controls are documented.
- [ ] **Management Review and Audit Reports**: Management review minutes and internal audit reports are maintained.
- [ ] **Certification Audit Readiness**: System meets third-party certification audit requirements.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Leading](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#5-leading)
