# Health and Safety Management File

[TOC]


Consolidated health and safety documentation including policies and incident records.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Policy Pack

Basic health and safety policy and procedures.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Policy Statement**: Health and safety policy statement is prepared.
- [ ] **Generic Risk Assessments**: Generic risk assessments and fire procedures are documented.
- [ ] **First Aid and PPE Records**: First aid arrangements and PPE issue records are maintained.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Identified](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#1-identified)

### 2. Managed File

Comprehensive documentation with COSHH and method statements.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **COSHH and Method Statements**: COSHH assessments for all substances and method statements are included.
- [ ] **Training and Equipment Records**: Training records and equipment inspection records are maintained.
- [ ] **Accident and Near-Miss Reports**: Accident and near-miss reports with investigation outcomes are documented.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Controlled](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#2-controlled)

**Developed by:**

- [Conduct Risk Assessment Programme](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/conduct-risk-assessment-programme.md)

### 3. Integrated System

Management system meeting ISO 45001 requirements.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Leadership and Participation Records**: Leadership commitment and worker participation records are documented.
- [ ] **Performance Monitoring**: Performance monitoring including leading indicators is operational.
- [ ] **Continuous Improvement Evidence**: Continuous improvement evidence and management review records are maintained.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Exemplary](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#5-exemplary)

**Developed by:**

- [Implement Proactive Safety Culture Programme](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/implement-proactive-safety-culture-programme.md)
