# Certification and Accreditation Portfolio

[TOC]


Organised evidence of professional certifications, accreditations, and quality scheme memberships.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Certificate File

Collected certificates with expiry date tracking.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Current Certificates Filed: Copies of all current certificates including PA1/PA6 are filed.
- [ ] Insurance Documents Tracked: Business insurance documents and expiry dates are tracked.
- [ ] CSCS and First Aid Included: CSCS cards and first aid certificates are included.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Compliant](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#2-compliant)

**Developed by:**

- [Establish Regulatory Compliance Framework](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/establish-regulatory-compliance-framework.md)
- [Manage Pesticide Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-pesticide-compliance.md)

### 2. Managed Portfolio

Structured portfolio with CPD records and renewals.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Certifications Grouped with Triggers: Certifications grouped by individual and category with renewal triggers.
- [ ] CPD Records Maintained: CPD records meeting CIH 30-hour annual requirement are maintained.
- [ ] Audit Evidence Packs: Pre-assembled evidence packs for external audits are prepared.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Certified](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#3-certified)
- [Workforce Development](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md) — [Progressive](../concerns/professional-practice/workforce-development.md#3-progressive)

**Developed by:**

- [Manage Workforce Development Programme](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/manage-workforce-development-programme.md)
- [Maintain Professional Certifications and Industry Accreditation](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/maintain-professional-certifications-and-industry-accreditation.md)

### 3. Integrated Accreditation System

Multi-standard compliance evidence mapped across schemes.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Cross-Mapped Evidence Requirements: Evidence requirements across BALI, GLOBALG.A.P., and ISO are cross-mapped.
- [ ] Duplication Eliminated: Duplication eliminated through shared evidence identification.
- [ ] Continuous Audit Readiness: Continuous audit readiness with automated evidence collection is maintained.

**Contributes to:**

- [Legal and Standards Framework](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md) — [Leading](../concerns/professional-practice/regulatory-compliance-standards.md#5-leading)

**Developed by:**

- [Lead Regulatory and Standards Development](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/lead-regulatory-and-standards-development.md)
