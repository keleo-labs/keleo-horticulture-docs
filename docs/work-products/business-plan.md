# Business Plan

[TOC]


Documented strategy covering market positioning, financial projections, and operational objectives.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Outline

Basic business concept with target market identified.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Business Concept Documentation**: Business concept and primary service offerings are documented.
- [ ] **Target Customer Identification**: Target customer segments and geographic service area are identified.
- [ ] **High-Level Financial Estimates**: High-level annual revenue and cost estimates are prepared.

**Contributes to:**

- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Planned](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#1-planned)

### 2. Structured

Comprehensive plan with market analysis and forecasts.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Market Analysis**: Market analysis using Oxford Economics and Defra sector data is incorporated.
- [ ] **Three-Year Financial Forecasts**: Three-year financial forecasts with quarterly cash flow are prepared.
- [ ] **Staffing and Equipment Plan**: Staffing plan, equipment requirements, and marketing strategy are documented.
- [ ] **Risk Assessment**: Risk assessment identifying threats and mitigations is included.

**Contributes to:**

- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Coordinated](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#2-coordinated)
- [Financial Management](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md) — [Budgeted](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md#2-budgeted)

**Developed by:**

- [Develop Business Plan](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/develop-business-plan.md)
- [Conduct Financial Planning and Performance Analysis](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/conduct-financial-planning-and-performance-analysis.md)

### 3. Strategic

Multi-year plan with scenario modelling.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Five-Year Plan with Scenarios**: Five-year plan with scenario modelling for market conditions is prepared.
- [ ] **Capital Investment Appraisal**: Capital investment appraisal and diversification strategies are detailed.
- [ ] **Industry Benchmarking Data**: Industry benchmarking data and measurable performance targets are incorporated.

**Contributes to:**

- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Strategic](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#5-strategic)
- [Financial Management](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md) — [Strategic](../concerns/professional-practice/financial-management.md#5-strategic)

**Developed by:**

- [Conduct Strategic Business Development](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/conduct-strategic-business-development.md)

### 4. Investor-Ready

Fully evidenced plan meeting external funding requirements.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Audited Financial Data**: Audited financial data and independent market research are included.
- [ ] **Governance Structure Evidence**: Governance structure and demonstrable track record evidence are documented.
- [ ] **Sensitivity and Exit Analysis**: Sensitivity analysis and exit or succession planning are incorporated.

**Contributes to:**

- [Enterprise Management](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md) — [Innovative](../concerns/professional-practice/business-operations-governance.md#6-innovative)
