# Risk Assessment Portfolio

[TOC]


Collection of documented risk assessments covering operational and environmental hazards.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Generic

Standard risk assessments for common activities.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Template-Based Assessments**: Template-based assessments for mowing, planting, and pruning are completed.
- [ ] **Likelihood-Severity Matrix**: Basic likelihood-severity matrix with generic controls is applied.
- [ ] **Standard COSHH Assessments**: COSHH assessments for standard chemical products are included.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Identified](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#1-identified)

### 2. Site-Specific

Tailored assessments for actual site conditions.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Site-Specific Hazard Documentation**: Site-specific hazard observations and control measures are documented.
- [ ] **Dynamic Risk Assessment Elements**: Dynamic risk assessment elements for weather and public access are included.
- [ ] **Individual COSHH Assessments**: Individual COSHH assessments for all chemical products used are documented.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Controlled](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#2-controlled)

**Developed by:**

- [Conduct Risk Assessment Programme](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/conduct-risk-assessment-programme.md)
- [Manage Pesticide Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-pesticide-compliance.md)

### 3. Comprehensive

Integrated risk portfolio with hierarchy-of-control analysis.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Full Risk Coverage**: All operational, environmental, financial, and reputational risks are covered.
- [ ] **Health Surveillance Protocols**: Health surveillance protocols and behavioural safety records are included.
- [ ] **Leading Indicator Tracking**: Leading indicator tracking and incident-triggered reviews are documented.

**Contributes to:**

- [Health & Safety Management](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md) — [Managed](../concerns/professional-practice/health-safety-management.md#3-managed)

**Developed by:**

- [Implement Proactive Safety Culture Programme](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/implement-proactive-safety-culture-programme.md)
