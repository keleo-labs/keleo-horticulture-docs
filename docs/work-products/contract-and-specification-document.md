# Contract and Specification Document

[TOC]


Formal agreement defining scope, standards, pricing, and terms for service delivery.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Quotation

Written scope and price for a defined package.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Scope and Pricing Breakdown: Work scope, materials, and pricing breakdown are specified.
- [ ] Duration and Payment Terms: Estimated duration, payment terms, and standard conditions are included.
- [ ] Exclusions and Limitations: Exclusions and limitations are clearly stated.

**Contributes to:**

- [Client & Stakeholder Management](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md) — [Responsive](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md#1-responsive)

### 2. Specification

Detailed technical specification with quality standards.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Plant and Material Specifications: Plant species, sizes, and material specifications are defined.
- [ ] Workmanship Standards: Workmanship standards referencing British Standards are included.
- [ ] Maintenance and Defect Liability: Maintenance regimes and defect liability periods are documented.

**Contributes to:**

- [Client & Stakeholder Management](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md) — [Managed](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md#2-managed)

**Developed by:**

- [Administer Contracts and Client Relationships](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/administer-contracts-and-client-relationships.md)
- [Develop Proactive Client Engagement Programme](../activities/professional-practice/plan-coordinate-operations/develop-proactive-client-engagement-programme.md)
- [Manage Quality Assurance and Standards Compliance](../activities/professional-practice/ensure-compliance-quality/manage-quality-assurance-and-standards-compliance.md)

### 3. Contract

Comprehensive contract with conditions and dispute resolution.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Conditions and Bill of Quantities: Conditions of engagement and detailed bill of quantities are included.
- [ ] Variation and Valuation Procedures: Variation, daywork, and interim valuation procedures are specified.
- [ ] Dispute Resolution Provisions: Dispute resolution provisions and retention mechanisms are documented.

**Contributes to:**

- [Client & Stakeholder Management](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md) — [Strategic](../concerns/professional-practice/client-stakeholder-management.md#4-strategic)
