# <i class="fa-solid fa-shield-virus" style="font-size:20px"></i> IPM Plan

[TOC]


Integrated plan coordinating pest, disease, and weed management tactics.

## Levels of Detail

### 1. Pest and Disease Register

Target organisms with identification and intervention options.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Target organisms listed**: Strategic objectives, implementation timeline, success criteria, and resource requirements defined with stakeholder approvals and regular review schedules established.
- [ ] **Monitoring methods specified**: Strategic objectives, implementation timeline, success criteria, and resource requirements defined with stakeholder approvals and regular review schedules established.
- [ ] **Intervention options catalogued**: Approved intervention options catalogued per target organism by internal or external auditors.

**Contributes to:**

- [IPM](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md) — [Monitored](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md#1-monitored)

**Developed by:**

- [Perform Pest and Disease Scouting](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/perform-pest-and-disease-scouting.md)

### 2. Threshold-Based Action Plan

Decision framework with economic injury levels and escalation.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **EILs**: Strategic objectives, implementation timeline, success criteria, and resource requirements defined with stakeholder approvals and regular review schedules established.
- [ ] **Action thresholds specify triggers**: Action thresholds specify population density triggering intervention by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Intervention sequences**: Preferred intervention sequences and escalation criteria defined by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Resistance monitoring baselines**: Resistance monitoring baselines established for active substances, with systematic data collection, trend analysis, and documented response protocols for threshold exceedances.

**Contributes to:**

- [IPM](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md) — [Thresholds Established](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md#2-thresholds-established)

**Developed by:**

- [Establish Action Thresholds for Pest Management](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/establish-action-thresholds-for-pest-management.md)

### 3. Integrated Management System

Comprehensive strategy integrating biological, cultural, and chemical tactics.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Biocontrol schedules integrated**: Biological control deployment schedules integrated with cultural practices, with implementation verified through direct observation, measurements, or documented evidence of operational effectiveness.
- [ ] **Chemical use justified with rotation**: Chemical use justification records maintained with resistance rotation plans by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Pollinator risk mitigated**: Pollinator risk mitigation measures documented for all applications by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Annual programme unified**: Annual programme unifies prevention, monitoring, and intervention tactics, with systematic data collection, trend analysis, and documented response protocols for threshold exceedances.

**Contributes to:**

- [IPM](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md) — [Integrated](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md#5-integrated)
- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Reduced](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#2-reduced)

**Developed by:**

- [Deploy Biological Control Agents](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/deploy-biological-control-agents.md)
- [Establish Action Thresholds for Pest Management](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/establish-action-thresholds-for-pest-management.md)
- [Manage Resistance in Pest Populations](../activities/biological-systems/protect-plants-ecosystems/manage-resistance-in-pest-populations.md)

### 4. Ecological Management Framework

Landscape-scale plan with conservation biocontrol habitat design.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Conservation habitat designed at landscape**: Conservation biocontrol habitat designed at landscape scale by internal or external auditors.
- [ ] **Pollinator corridor mapping included**: Pollinator corridor mapping and beneficial arthropod monitoring included, with systematic data collection, trend analysis, and documented response protocols for threshold exceedances.
- [ ] **Agroecosystem outcome evaluation conducted**: Agroecosystem-level outcome evaluation conducted beyond field boundaries, using established criteria and documented methodology with results validated by qualified practitioners.

**Contributes to:**

- [IPM](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md) — [Ecological](../concerns/biological-systems/plant-protection.md#6-ecological)
- [Environmental Sustainability](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md) — [Positive](../concerns/biological-systems/sustainability-environmental-stewardship.md#4-positive)

**Developed by:**
