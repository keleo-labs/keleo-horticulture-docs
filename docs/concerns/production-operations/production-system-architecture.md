# <i class="fa-solid fa-industry" style="font-size:20px"></i> CEA System Design

[TOC]


The physical infrastructure, cultivation methods, and technological systems employed to produce horticultural outputs.

## Production System Architecture Journey

Narrative describing the transformation from traditional greenhouse design to autonomous AI-managed production.

1. Traditional greenhouse design relied on empirical rules and local craft knowledge, limiting scalability and efficiency.
2. The CEA market forecasted to reach $12.77 billion by 2026 demands quantitative design frameworks integrating structural mechanics, energy performance, and digital simulation.
3. Reconciling structural integrity under environmental loads with passive climate control, crop compatibility, and financial constraints across diverse system types.
4. Fully autonomous AI-managed production systems outperform experienced human growers across complete crop cycles, as demonstrated by the Wageningen Autonomous Greenhouse Challenge.
5. Optimized production architectures are documented and replicated, establishing industry benchmarks for efficiency, sustainability, and autonomous operation.
