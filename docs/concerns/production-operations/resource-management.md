# <i class="fa-solid fa-droplet" style="font-size:20px"></i> Water-Energy-Nutrient Nexus

[TOC]


The efficient acquisition, allocation, and utilization of water, nutrients, energy, and materials to minimize environmental impact while maximizing productivity.

## Resource Management Transformation

Narrative describing the progression from linear resource consumption to regenerative circular systems.

1. Horticultural operations consume significant water, nutrients, and energy resources, with conventional linear resource flows generating waste and environmental degradation.
2. Precision measurement, sensor-driven budgeting, IoT-enabled smart irrigation achieving 25-50% water reduction, and variable rate technology transforming resource management from empirical to data-driven.
3. Closed-loop nutrient recovery reduces commercial fertilizer use by 40%, aeroponic recirculation cuts freshwater input by 74%, and regenerative outcomes create net-positive environmental impact verified through lifecycle assessment.
