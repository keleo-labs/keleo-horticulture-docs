# <i class="fa-solid fa-biohazard" style="font-size:20px"></i> Biosecurity

[TOC]


The prevention, detection, and containment of invasive species and pathogens to protect managed and wild ecosystems.

## Biosecurity Defence Narrative

Three-act narrative for biosecurity system development.

1. The UK imports 18.6 million tonnes of plants annually, each consignment a potential pathway for pest introduction, with Xylella fastidiosa threatening 5.5 billion euros in EU production value.
2. LAMP diagnostics, eDNA surveillance, risk-based import assessment, and structured quarantine protocols create layered biosecurity defences from border to growing environment.
3. Biosecurity systems achieve rapid detection and containment, international cooperation shares intelligence, and simulation exercises build institutional readiness for novel threats.
