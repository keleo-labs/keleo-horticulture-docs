# <i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" style="font-size:20px"></i> IPM

[TOC]


The prevention, detection, and control of weeds, pathogens, and insect pests through integrated management strategies.

## Plant Protection Journey

STAR narrative for the evolution of integrated plant protection.

1. Pest resistance to chemical controls accelerates across 600+ insect species, regulatory restrictions reduce available active substances, and pollinator health must be co-managed with pest suppression.
2. Transition from calendar-based chemical application to evidence-based IPM integrating biological, cultural, physical, and chemical tactics within a threshold-driven decision framework.
3. Deploy AI-powered monitoring, biological control agents, robotic weeding, resistance management protocols, and pollinator-safe intervention timing coordinated through documented IPM strategies.
4. Chemical pesticide use approaches zero, beneficial organism populations are self-sustaining, pest outbreaks are self-regulating through established trophic interactions, and pollinator populations are stable or increasing.
