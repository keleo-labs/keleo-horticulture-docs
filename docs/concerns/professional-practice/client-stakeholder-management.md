# <i class="fa-solid fa-handshake" style="font-size:20px"></i> Client & Stakeholder Management

[TOC]


Systematic management of client relationships and stakeholder engagement in horticultural services.

## Client Management Context

Systematic client relationship development from enquiry handling to strategic partnership.

1. UK households spent 5.8 billion GBP on professional horticultural services in 2023, creating a substantial market requiring professional client engagement spanning private, commercial, and public sector clients.
2. Establish systematic client management from initial enquiry through contract delivery to long-term relationship development, meeting Landscape Institute and BALI professional standards.
3. Implement standardized enquiry handling, specification writing, contract administration, progress reporting, and client feedback systems aligned with industry body Codes of Conduct.
4. Improved client satisfaction, increased referral and repeat business rates, and strategic partnerships with key accounts driving sustainable business growth.

## States

### 1. Responsive

Client enquiries handled through standardized processes.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Enquiry Handling Processes: Enquiry handling processes covering initial response and quotation delivery are demonstrably standardized.
- [ ] Client Requirements Capture: Client requirements including scope, budget, and aesthetic preferences are demonstrably captured through structured consultation.
- [ ] Written Quotations: Written quotations specifying scope, exclusions, pricing, and payment terms are demonstrably provided.
- [ ] Terms and Conditions: Terms and conditions covering liability, cancellation, and dispute resolution are demonstrably communicated before work commences.
- [ ] Response Time Tracking: Response times for enquiries and quotations are demonstrably tracked against service levels.

### 2. Managed

Client relationships systematically maintained throughout delivery.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Contract Administration: Contract administration covering variation orders, interim valuations, and practical completion is demonstrably operational.
- [ ] Progress Communication: Progress communication providing regular updates on schedule and issues is demonstrably standardized.
- [ ] Post-Completion Handover: Post-completion handover including maintenance guidance and warranty information is demonstrably delivered.
- [ ] Client Records: Client records capturing project history, preferences, and communication are demonstrably maintained.
- [ ] Complaint Handling: Complaint handling procedures with defined escalation paths are demonstrably functioning.
