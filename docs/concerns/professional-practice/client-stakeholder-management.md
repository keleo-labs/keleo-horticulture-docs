# <i class="fa-solid fa-handshake" style="font-size:20px"></i> Client & Stakeholder Management

[TOC]


Systematic management of client relationships and stakeholder engagement in horticultural services.

## Client Management Context

Systematic client relationship development from enquiry handling to strategic partnership.

1. UK households spent 5.8 billion GBP on professional horticultural services in 2023, creating a substantial market requiring professional client engagement spanning private, commercial, and public sector clients.
2. Establish systematic client management from initial enquiry through contract delivery to long-term relationship development, meeting Landscape Institute and BALI professional standards.
3. Implement standardized enquiry handling, specification writing, contract administration, progress reporting, and client feedback systems aligned with industry body Codes of Conduct.
4. Improved client satisfaction, increased referral and repeat business rates, and strategic partnerships with key accounts driving sustainable business growth.

## States

### 1. Responsive

Client enquiries handled through standardized processes.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Enquiry Response Standardized**: Enquiry handling processes covering initial response (acknowledgment within 24 hours), site visits (scheduled within 1 week for commercial clients), and quotation delivery (detailed proposals within 2 weeks) are demonstrably standardized.
- [ ] **Requirements Captured Systematically**: Client requirements including scope (areas to be managed, plant types, maintenance frequency), budget constraints (capital expenditure limits, annual maintenance budgets), and aesthetic preferences (design styles, plant palettes) are demonstrably captured through structured consultation meetings with documented specifications.
- [ ] **Written Quotations Provided**: Written quotations specifying scope of works (planting schedules, maintenance operations, materials), exclusions (client-supplied plants, access constraints, underground services), pricing (itemized breakdown, payment stages), and payment terms (deposit requirements, credit periods) are demonstrably provided.
- [ ] **Terms and Conditions Communicated**: Terms and conditions covering liability limitations (force majeure, plant establishment guarantees), cancellation policies (notice periods, cancellation charges), and dispute resolution (arbitration procedures, professional indemnity cover) are demonstrably communicated before work commences.
- [ ] **Response Times Monitored**: Response times for enquiries (target 24-hour acknowledgment) and quotations (target 2-week turnaround for standard work, 4 weeks for complex design-build projects) are demonstrably tracked against service levels with performance reporting.

### 2. Managed

Client relationships systematically maintained throughout delivery.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Contract Administration Operational**: Contract administration covering variation orders (scope changes priced and approved before execution), interim valuations (monthly billing for multi-phase contracts), and practical completion (formal handover with defects list) is demonstrably operational.
- [ ] **Progress Updates Standardized**: Progress communication providing regular updates on schedule (milestone achievement, weather delays, programme adjustments), quality (workmanship standards, plant health inspections), and issues (access problems, client decisions required) is demonstrably standardized.
- [ ] **Post-Completion Handover Delivered**: Post-completion handover including maintenance guidance (seasonal care calendars, irrigation schedules, pruning requirements), warranty information (plant establishment guarantees, defect rectification periods), and as-built documentation (planting plans, material specifications) is demonstrably delivered.
- [ ] **Client Records Maintained**: Client records capturing project history (contracts delivered, variations approved, final account values), preferences (favored plant genera, material choices, design styles), and communication (meeting notes, correspondence, decisions made) are demonstrably maintained in CRM systems or client files.
- [ ] **Complaint Handling Functional**: Complaint handling procedures with defined escalation paths (site manager → contracts manager → director), response timescales (acknowledgment within 24 hours, resolution plan within 5 working days), and resolution tracking are demonstrably functioning.

### 3. Proactive

Client value actively developed beyond initial delivery.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Maintenance Programmes Proposed**: Maintenance programme proposals extending client relationships beyond initial installation through annual maintenance contracts (mowing, pruning, seasonal bedding), long-term care packages (tree surgery, irrigation system servicing), or consultancy retainers are demonstrably offered.
- [ ] **Seasonal Advisory Communications Issued**: Seasonal advisory communications providing timely horticultural guidance (spring fertilizer application reminders, summer drought management advice, autumn bulb planting recommendations) are demonstrably issued to maintenance contract clients.
- [ ] **Relationship Quality Metrics Tracked**: Referral and repeat business rates are demonstrably tracked as relationship quality measures with analysis showing percentage of revenue from existing clients, referral source attribution, and Net Promoter Score trends.
- [ ] **Client Feedback Integrated**: Client feedback is demonstrably incorporated into service improvement through structured review (post-project surveys analyzed quarterly, common themes addressed in process updates, positive feedback shared with teams for recognition).
- [ ] **Public Engagement Conducted**: Public engagement activities including community planting events (volunteering programmes for habitat creation), educational workshops (school garden projects, public pruning demonstrations), or open garden days demonstrating exemplar projects are demonstrably conducted.

### 4. Strategic

Stakeholder relationships drive business growth.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Key Account Management Structured**: Key account management for major clients including local authorities (parks departments, highways landscape maintenance), conservation bodies (National Trust, Wildlife Trusts), or estate management companies is demonstrably structured with dedicated account managers and annual business reviews.
- [ ] **Multi-Stakeholder Coordination Effective**: Multi-stakeholder project coordination spanning architects (design team liaison), engineers (drainage and structural integration), ecologists (biodiversity net gain requirements), and planning authorities (discharge of landscape conditions) is demonstrably effective with documented communication protocols.
- [ ] **Portfolio Analysis Informing Strategy**: Client portfolio analysis informing service development (identifying growth sectors like biodiversity net gain, green infrastructure) and market positioning (targeting private estates, commercial developments, public realm) is demonstrably conducted annually with strategic planning linkage.
- [ ] **Community Benefit Engagement Evidenced**: Public engagement contributing to community benefit through biodiversity awareness (pollinator-friendly planting education, habitat creation volunteering), urban greening advocacy (promoting benefits of street trees, green roofs), or horticultural therapy programmes is demonstrably evidenced.
- [ ] **Professional Reputation Management Active**: Professional reputation management through case study publication (BALI Landscape journal articles, RHS journal features), award submissions (BALI National Landscape Awards, RHS Chelsea exhibits), or conference presentations (industry speaking engagements) is demonstrably active.
