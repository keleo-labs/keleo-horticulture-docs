# <i class="fa-solid fa-helmet-safety" style="font-size:20px"></i> Health & Safety Management

[TOC]


Systematic identification, assessment, and control of occupational health and safety risks.

## Health and Safety Context

Safety maturity from hazard identification to exemplary safety culture in horticulture.

1. Horticultural work presents a distinctive risk profile combining outdoor exposure, manual handling of heavy materials, powered machinery operation, COSHH-regulated chemical handling, and working at height in arboricultural operations.
2. Establish comprehensive safety management from hazard identification through controls implementation to proactive safety culture, meeting statutory requirements and exceeding sector benchmarks.
3. Implement structured risk assessment programmes, COSHH assessments for all substances, safe systems of work, health surveillance, and behavioural safety observations with leading indicator tracking.
4. Sustained zero serious harm record, ISO 45001 accreditation, and safety practices adopted by the sector as benchmark standards.

## States

### 1. Identified

Hazards and legal obligations formally recognized.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Hazard Register: A register of all hazards including manual handling, machinery, chemicals, and environmental exposure is demonstrably compiled.
- [ ] Statutory Obligations Identification: Statutory obligations under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974 and Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations are demonstrably identified.
- [ ] COSHH Substance Listing: COSHH-regulated substances are demonstrably listed with safety data sheets obtained and accessible.
- [ ] High-Risk Activity Categorization: High-risk activities including chainsaw operation and working at height are demonstrably identified and categorized.
- [ ] Lone Working Risk Recognition: Lone working situations and their associated risks are demonstrably recognized and documented.

### 2. Controlled

Risk controls systematically implemented following hierarchy.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Written Risk Assessments: Written risk assessments following the hierarchy of control are demonstrably completed for all identified hazards.
- [ ] COSHH Assessments: COSHH assessments for all chemical products are demonstrably current with control measures specified.
- [ ] PPE Provision and Training: PPE provision, training, and replacement procedures are demonstrably operational with records maintained.
- [ ] Safe Systems of Work: Safe systems of work for high-risk activities including chainsaw use and pesticide application are demonstrably documented.
- [ ] First Aid and Emergency Procedures: First aid provision, emergency procedures, and RIDDOR-compliant accident reporting are demonstrably in place.
- [ ] Machinery Safety Procedures: Machinery safety procedures including pre-use checks and operator competency verification are demonstrably enforced.

### 3. Managed

Safety performance monitored through data analysis.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Accident Data Analysis: Accident and incident data including near-miss reporting is demonstrably analysed for trends and root causes.
- [ ] Health Surveillance: Health surveillance for workers exposed to vibration, noise, or sensitizing substances is demonstrably conducted.
- [ ] Safety Inspections: Safety inspections of work sites and equipment are demonstrably carried out at defined frequencies.
- [ ] Toolbox Talks and Briefings: Toolbox talks and safety briefings are demonstrably delivered before commencing work on new sites.
- [ ] Safety Training Records: Safety training records demonstrably evidence current competencies for all personnel.

**Evidenced by:**

- [Risk Assessment Portfolio](../../work-products/risk-assessment-portfolio.md) — [Comprehensive](../../work-products/risk-assessment-portfolio.md#3-comprehensive)

### 4. Proactive

Safety culture prevents incidents before occurrence.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Leading Safety Indicators: Leading safety indicators including training completion and near-miss reporting rates are demonstrably tracked.
- [ ] Behavioural Safety Observations: Behavioural safety observations identifying at-risk practices are demonstrably conducted.
- [ ] Worker Engagement in Safety: Worker engagement in safety improvement through consultation or safety committees is demonstrably active.
- [ ] Mental Health and Wellbeing Support: Mental health and wellbeing support addressing physically demanding horticultural work is demonstrably provided.
- [ ] Benchmark-Exceeding Safety Performance: Safety performance demonstrably exceeds sector benchmarks with continuous improvement trends.

**Progressed by:**

### 5. Exemplary

Safety practices set industry standards and benchmarks.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Safety Management Accreditation: Safety management system accreditation such as ISO 45001 is demonstrably achieved.
- [ ] Sector Safety Guidance Contribution: Contribution to sector safety guidance through industry body participation is demonstrably evidenced.
- [ ] Zero Serious Harm Record: Zero serious harm track record maintained over a sustained period is demonstrably documented.
- [ ] Safety Innovation: Safety innovation including novel equipment or procedures reducing risk is demonstrably implemented.
- [ ] Benchmark Safety Practices: The organization's safety practices are demonstrably referenced by industry bodies as benchmarks.
