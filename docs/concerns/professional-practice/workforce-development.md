# <i class="fa-solid fa-graduation-cap" style="font-size:20px"></i> Workforce Development

[TOC]


Structured development of horticultural workforce capability through qualifications and career pathways.

## Workforce Development Context

Structured workforce development from skills audit to sector-leading practice.

1. The UK horticultural workforce has an average decision-maker age of 55, predominantly informal skill acquisition, and a 3% supervisory ratio that constrains operational growth.
2. Build structured development pathways from Level 2 apprenticeship through RHS qualifications to Chartered professional status, while addressing the supervisory capacity gap.
3. Implement individual development plans, apprenticeship programmes aligned with Skills England standards, CPD tracking against CIH requirements, and supervisory training initiatives.
4. Measurable improvement in qualification attainment rates, internal promotion, employee retention, and organizational capability, with the enterprise contributing to sector workforce standards.

## States

### 1. Identified

Workforce skill requirements formally mapped.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Competency Documentation: Current workforce competencies and qualification levels are demonstrably documented for all roles.
- [ ] Skill Gap Analysis: Skill gaps against business requirements and AHDB Skills Survey benchmarks are demonstrably identified.
- [ ] Recruitment Challenge Analysis: Recruitment challenges including hard-to-fill roles and seasonal labour dependencies are demonstrably analysed.
- [ ] Qualification Pathway Mapping: Qualification pathways from entry-level through Chartered status are demonstrably understood and documented.
- [ ] Demographic Impact Assessment: The impact of workforce demographics including age profile and succession risk is demonstrably assessed.

### 2. Structured

Development programmes formally established and resourced.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Apprenticeship Programmes: Apprenticeship programmes aligned with Skills England standards ST0225 and ST0226 are demonstrably offered or planned.
- [ ] Individual Development Plans: Individual development plans linking employees to RHS, City and Guilds, or NPTC qualification pathways are demonstrably in place.
- [ ] Supervisory Development: Supervisory development addressing the industry 3% supervisor gap through structured leadership training is demonstrably operational.
- [ ] Induction Programmes: Induction programmes for new starters covering safety, compliance, and technical fundamentals are demonstrably standardized.
- [ ] Mentoring Arrangements: Mentoring arrangements pairing experienced practitioners with trainees are demonstrably functioning.

### 3. Progressive

Workforce capability measurably advancing.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Qualification Attainment Tracking: Qualification attainment rates across the workforce are demonstrably tracked and trending upward.
- [ ] CPD Participation: CPD participation meeting CIH benchmarks of 30 hours annually for professional staff is demonstrably evidenced.
- [ ] Internal Promotion: Internal promotion from operative to supervisory and management roles is demonstrably occurring.
- [ ] Workforce Diversity: Workforce diversity across age, gender, and background is demonstrably improving against sector norms.
- [ ] Training Investment Measurement: Training investment as a proportion of revenue is demonstrably measured and benchmarked.
- [ ] Employee Retention Improvement: Employee retention rates are demonstrably improved through development programme participation.
