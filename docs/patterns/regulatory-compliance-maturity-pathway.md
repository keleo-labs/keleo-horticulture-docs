### Prerequisites

Decision to operate professionally with compliance commitment.

1. Horticultural operations face a complex regulatory landscape spanning pesticide certification with no grandfather rights, post-Brexit phytosanitary controls, tiered environmental permitting, and evolving sustainability requirements across multiple certification schemes.

### Awareness and Basic Compliance

All obligations identified and mandatory requirements met.

1. A structured compliance framework systematically identifies all obligations, obtains mandatory certifications, implements COSHH assessments and risk controls, registers waste exemptions, and classifies plant material sources against BTOM categories.

### Certification and Systematic Compliance

Industry accreditations achieved and proactive compliance established.

1. The enterprise achieves industry accreditation through BALI, GLOBALG.A.P., and environmental certifications, implements horizon scanning for regulatory change, obtains specialist certifications beyond minimum requirements, and establishes proactive safety culture with leading indicator tracking.

### Leadership and Influence

Compliance practices set sector benchmarks and influence regulation.

1. The enterprise sets compliance benchmarks adopted by peers, achieves Chartered professional status, contributes to regulatory development through standards committees, and publishes compliance case studies advancing sector knowledge.
