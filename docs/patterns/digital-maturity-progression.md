### Prerequisites

Production system operational with manual management and budget.

1. Horticultural operations rely on manual monitoring, experience-based decisions, and reactive management, limiting the ability to optimize multi-parameter growing conditions or predict crop outcomes.

### Instrument and Connect

Sensor networks deployed with interoperable data connectivity.

1. Dense sensor networks deploying optical, electrochemical, and spectral modalities across production zones establish the data foundation, while ISOBUS and AgGateway standards enable cross-vendor interoperability.

### Analyze and Automate

ML models trained and automated control systems deployed.

1. Training ML models with limited labelled data, achieving cross-vendor system integration, and building organizational trust in automated decision-making represent the primary challenges of digital advancement.

### Predict and Autonomize

Digital twins, predictive models, and autonomous control operational.

1. Digital twins simulate production behaviour with validated fidelity, CNNs achieve 98.32% disease detection accuracy, and AI systems manage complete crop cycles outperforming experienced human growers in the Wageningen Autonomous Greenhouse Challenge.
2. Open agricultural data standards, published competition results, and shared digital twin architectures accelerate industry-wide digital transformation from precision agriculture to intelligent agricultural ecosystems.
