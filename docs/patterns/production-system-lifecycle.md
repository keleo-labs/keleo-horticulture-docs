### Prerequisites

Production objectives, site assessment, and budget approval.

1. Traditional greenhouse design relies on empirical rules and local craft knowledge, limiting scalability, energy efficiency, and the capacity for progressive automation.

### Design and Engineering

System architecture designed and equipment procurement initiated.

1. The CEA market growth to $12.77 billion demands quantitative design frameworks integrating structural mechanics, energy performance, and digital simulation into unified production architectures.

### Construction and Commissioning

Physical structures built, systems installed, and performance validated.

1. Construction to BS EN 13031-1 tolerances, commissioning under representative loads, sensor calibration baselines, and staff training must all converge before production cycles can begin.

### Operation and Optimization

Production cycles executed with progressive data-driven optimization.

1. Data-driven refinements including DRL-based climate control achieving 57% energy reduction, VRT with 20-45% nutrient efficiency gains, and digital twin simulation with 15-20% yield improvements transform operations from empirical to evidence-based management.

### Integration and Autonomy

Autonomous control with circular resources and predictive maintenance.

1. Autonomous systems validated through the Wageningen Challenge, predictive maintenance reducing costs 15-20%, and circular resource recovery achieving 74% freshwater reduction establish replicable industry benchmarks for next-generation horticultural production.
