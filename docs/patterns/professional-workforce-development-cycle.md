# Professional Workforce Development Cycle

[TOC]


Continuous cycle of workforce capability assessment, development, and advancement.

### Prerequisites

Sector skills intelligence reviewed and training budget established.

1. The horticultural sector faces structural workforce challenges including an average decision-maker age of 55, predominantly informal skill acquisition, a 3% supervisory ratio, and limited career progression pathways in smaller businesses.

### Capability Assessment

Current workforce profile audited against requirements.

1. Audit current qualifications and competency levels against role requirements and sector benchmarks, identifying skill gaps, succession risks, and demographic vulnerabilities to inform structured development investment.

### Structured Development

Apprenticeships, qualifications, and mentoring programmes active.

1. Establish apprenticeship programmes aligned with Skills England ST0225 and ST0226 standards, implement individual development plans targeting RHS and professional body qualifications, deliver supervisory development addressing the industry capacity gap, and facilitate CPD activities meeting CIH and Landscape Institute requirements.

### Advancement and Contribution

Workforce outcomes measured and sector standards advanced.

1. Qualification attainment rates exceed sector benchmarks, internal promotion from operative to supervisory roles is routine, training investment generates measurable returns, and the organization contributes to workforce standard development through published case studies and educational partnerships.
