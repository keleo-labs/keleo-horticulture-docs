### Prerequisites

Organisational commitment, baseline data systems, and certification targets identified.

1. Horticultural operations lack systematic environmental accounting, depend on finite peat resources, and generate unmeasured emissions. Without quantified baselines, reduction targets and regenerative aspirations have no evidential foundation.

### Measure and Understand Impact

Baseline environmental footprint established using standardised methods.

1. Environmental footprint measurement reveals emission hotspots, with hybrid LCA demonstrating that conventional methods underestimate horticultural emissions by approximately 83%. Biodiversity baseline surveys establish the pre-intervention ecological condition.

### Reduce and Transition

Quantified reduction targets set and peat-free transition implemented.

1. Peat-free transition is implemented systematically beginning with validated crop applications. IPM adoption reduces chemical pesticide use. Renewable energy and closed-loop water systems reduce resource intensity. Environmental certification provides third-party verification.

### Achieve Positive and Regenerative Outcomes

Operations generate net positive environmental and ecological benefit.

1. Biodiversity net gain of at least 10% is achieved and secured. Soil carbon stocks demonstrate net sequestration. Circular economy principles govern material flows. The horticultural operation contributes to sustainability advocacy and nature-based solutions.
