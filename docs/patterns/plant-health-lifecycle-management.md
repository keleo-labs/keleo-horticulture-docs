### Prerequisites

Plant material sources and monitoring infrastructure verified.

1. Plant production operates with basic visual inspection, limited provenance tracking, and reactive interventions. Growing environments are managed through experience-based recipes without systematic monitoring or evidence-based substrate formulation.

### Establish Identity and Baseline Health

Plant material verified, baseline health established, environments prepared.

1. Molecular verification reveals mislabelled stock and latent viral infections. Rhizosphere profiling shows microbial communities that could be actively managed. The imperative to verify identity and establish baseline health demands a systematic approach.

### Monitor and Optimise Performance

Structured monitoring drives evidence-based performance optimisation.

1. Integrating phenotyping data with environmental monitoring while transitioning to peat-free substrates. Establishing action thresholds for pest management requires multi-season calibration data. Balancing productivity optimisation with substrate sustainability presents complex trade-offs.

### Build Resilience and Adaptive Capacity

Stress tolerance built through cultivar selection and IPM integration.

1. Plant production achieves predictable quality through resilient cultivars, integrated pest management, and dynamically controlled environments. Environmental footprint measurement reveals emission hotspots and quantifies reduction progress.

### Achieve Regenerative Outcomes

Systems generate net positive environmental and genetic outcomes.

1. Germplasm conservation and microbiome co-evolution build transgenerational resilience. Knowledge from each production cycle improves subsequent protocols. The system generates net positive biodiversity, carbon sequestration, and ecosystem services.
