### Prerequisites

IPM strategy, monitoring infrastructure, and biocontrol supply established.

1. Production systems face diverse pest complexes with accelerating resistance to chemical controls, tightening regulation of active substances, and growing recognition that pollinator health must be co-managed with pest suppression.

### Detect and Quantify Threats

Systematic scouting quantifies pest, disease, and beneficial populations.

1. Detect pests at the earliest stage and quantify populations against action thresholds while simultaneously surveilling for regulated and exotic organisms through biosecurity monitoring programmes.

### Assess Risk and Determine Response

Monitoring data compared against thresholds to determine intervention.

1. Compare monitoring data against thresholds, evaluate pest trajectory and crop vulnerability, assess pollinator activity and beneficial organism presence, and produce documented decisions with rationale for intervention or continued monitoring.

### Implement Proportionate Intervention

Biological and integrated controls deployed proportionally.

1. Deploy biological controls as first-line response, implement cultural and mechanical interventions, apply chemical intervention only when alternatives are insufficient using precision technology and resistance management rotation.

### Evaluate and Adapt

Season-end review refines thresholds, interventions, and habitat.

1. Season-end evaluation quantifies IPM effectiveness, resistance monitoring confirms mode-of-action diversity maintained, threshold values and intervention hierarchies are revised based on evidence, and conservation biocontrol habitat effectiveness is evaluated.
