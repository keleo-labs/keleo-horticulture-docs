# Resource Optimization Cycle

[TOC]


Iterative progression from measurement through circularity and regeneration.

### Prerequisites

Resource measurement infrastructure installed and baseline data available.

1. Resource measurement infrastructure is operational but consumption patterns are not yet understood, budgets are absent, and efficiency improvements remain unquantified.

### Measure and Plan

Resource consumption quantified and allocation budgets established.

1. Water, nutrient, and energy consumption are disaggregated across production zones, revealing spatial variability and inefficiency patterns that inform precision budgeting and conservation strategies.

### Conserve and Optimize

Conservation strategies and precision technology deployed.

1. Regulated deficit irrigation, LED lighting replacement, automated nutrient dosing, and variable rate technology collectively transform resource management from empirical scheduling to sensor-driven precision delivery.

### Close Loops and Regenerate

Circular recovery systems operational with regenerative outcomes verified.

1. Closed-loop phosphorus recovery reduces fertilizer use by 40%, aeroponic recirculation cuts freshwater input by 74%, and regenerative outcomes including carbon sequestration and biodiversity enhancement are independently verified through lifecycle assessment.
