# Professional Practice

[TOC]


Interpersonal, ethical, communication, and teamwork skills required for effective collaboration, client relations, and professional conduct.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Demonstrate reliability, punctuality, and positive attitude; follow instructions accurately and work safely; communicate clearly with team members and supervisors. |
| 2 | Applies | Provide excellent customer service (active listening, responsiveness, problem-solving); maintain confidentiality and discretion (especially in private estates or sensitive contexts); collaborate effectively in multi-disciplinary teams (horticulturists, engineers, ecologists). |
| 3 | Masters | Lead teams, manage volunteers, and coordinate crew activities; negotiate with clients, stakeholders, and regulators; present complex technical information to non-specialist audiences (reports, workshops, public talks). |
| 4 | Adapts | Resolve conflicts and manage difficult interpersonal situations; uphold professional ethics in challenging contexts (competing interests, resource constraints); represent the organization at industry events, public forums, or media engagements. |
| 5 | Innovating | Contribute to professional standards development (RHS, Landscape Institute, industry associations); mentor early-career professionals through formal programs or apprenticeships; advocate for the horticultural profession in policy, education, or public discourse. |
