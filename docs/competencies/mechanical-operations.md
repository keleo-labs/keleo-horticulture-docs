# Mechanical Operations

[TOC]


Proficiency in operating, maintaining, and troubleshooting horticultural machinery, hand tools, and construction equipment.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Safely operate hand tools (spades, rakes, pruners, loppers, saws); perform basic tool maintenance (cleaning, sharpening, oiling); understand safety protocols (PPE, manual handling, hazard awareness). |
| 2 | Applies | Operate powered equipment with certification (mowers, brush-cutters, chainsaws: NPTC/LANTRA); conduct pre-operational checks and minor repairs (changing blades, adjusting tension); use abrasive wheel equipment for cutting hard materials (paving, stone). |
| 3 | Masters | Operate complex machinery (tractors with implements, precision sprayers, excavators); diagnose mechanical faults and perform field repairs; calibrate equipment for accurate application rates. |
| 4 | Adapts | Modify or fabricate tools/equipment for specialized applications; train others in safe machinery operation and maintenance; manage machinery fleets (procurement, scheduling, lifecycle cost optimization). |
| 5 | Innovating | Collaborate with manufacturers to develop improved equipment designs; implement autonomous machinery systems (robotic mowers, driverless sprayers); conduct research on ergonomic tool design or operational efficiency. |
