# Business & Project Management

[TOC]


Ability to plan, budget, coordinate, and deliver horticultural projects or operations on time, within budget, and to client specifications.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Understand project scope, deliverables, and constraints; follow task instructions and report progress accurately; awareness of budget constraints and cost implications. |
| 2 | Applies | Develop basic project plans (task lists, timelines, resource assignments); track labor hours, material costs, and budget variances; communicate effectively with clients, suppliers, and team members. |
| 3 | Masters | Manage complex projects with multiple stakeholders, dependencies, and risks; prepare detailed budgets with contingency planning and cost-benefit analysis; negotiate contracts, manage procurement, and coordinate subcontractors. |
| 4 | Adapts | Oversee portfolio of concurrent projects with resource leveling and prioritization; implement project management software (Gantt charts, critical path, earned value); conduct post-project reviews and continuous improvement initiatives. |
| 5 | Innovating | Lead strategic business development (new markets, partnerships, service offerings); develop proprietary project management methodologies or tools; mentor others in business and project management; contribute to professional standards. |
