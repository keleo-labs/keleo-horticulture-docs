# Growing Media Management

[TOC]


Expertise in soil science, substrate formulation, rhizosphere dynamics, and nutrient chemistry to create and maintain optimal root environments.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Distinguish between soil types (sand, silt, clay) based on texture; understand the concept of pH and its effect on nutrient availability; recognize signs of poor drainage or waterlogging. |
| 2 | Applies | Conduct soil/substrate testing using field kits or interpret laboratory results; amend soils based on test results (liming, gypsum, organic matter); formulate basic peat-free growing mixes (coco coir, compost, perlite). |
| 3 | Masters | Design substrate recipes optimized for specific crops and production systems; manage fertility in soilless systems (EC/pH control, nutrient solution formulation); understand cation exchange capacity and nutrient retention dynamics. |
| 4 | Adapts | Troubleshoot complex substrate-related problems (salt accumulation, micronutrient deficiencies, pathogen buildup); integrate organic amendments to support beneficial microbial communities; design closed-loop nutrient recovery systems. |
| 5 | Innovating | Research novel substrate materials from waste streams (biochar, mushroom compost, agricultural by-products); develop proprietary substrate formulations for competitive advantage; contribute to scientific literature on rhizosphere ecology and nutrient dynamics. |
