# Environmental Stewardship

[TOC]


Commitment to sustainable practices, biodiversity conservation, carbon management, circular economy principles, and regenerative outcomes.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Awareness of environmental regulations (water quality, pesticide drift, waste disposal); understanding of basic ecology (food webs, pollination, nutrient cycles); recognize the importance of habitat conservation and resource efficiency. |
| 2 | Applies | Implement best management practices (BMPs) to reduce nutrient runoff, erosion, or pollution; create wildlife-friendly habitats (insect hotels, hedgerows, ponds, meadows); track and report environmental metrics (water use, waste generation, energy consumption). |
| 3 | Masters | Design operations for net-zero or net-positive environmental outcomes; integrate renewable energy (solar, wind, biogas) into production systems; develop circular economy strategies (compost, substrate recycling, waste valorization). |
| 4 | Adapts | Conduct life cycle assessments (LCA) to quantify environmental footprints and identify hotspots; lead carbon sequestration initiatives (biochar, agroforestry, soil organic matter building); navigate complex regulatory frameworks (carbon credits, biodiversity net gain, environmental permitting). |
| 5 | Innovating | Research regenerative horticulture practices or ecosystem restoration techniques; advocate for policy changes to support sustainable agriculture and climate action; develop industry standards for environmental performance or circular economy. |
