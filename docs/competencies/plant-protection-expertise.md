# Plant Protection Expertise

[TOC]


Knowledge and skills for integrated pest management, including pest/disease identification, biological controls, and safe pesticide application.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Recognize common pest groups (aphids, caterpillars, fungal diseases, weeds); understand IPM principles (prevention, monitoring, thresholds, least-risk controls); awareness of legal requirements for pesticide use and safety. |
| 2 | Applies | Identify specific pest/disease species using field guides or reference materials; conduct systematic scouting and record pest counts using monitoring protocols; apply pesticides safely with appropriate certification (PA1/PA6 in UK). |
| 3 | Masters | Design IPM programs tailored to specific crops, growing conditions, and risk profiles; deploy biological controls (predatory insects, entomopathogenic nematodes, microbial pesticides); interpret pest forecasting models (degree-days, population dynamics, epidemiology). |
| 4 | Adapts | Manage resistance development through chemical rotation, mode-of-action diversity, and refuge strategies; integrate habitat manipulation to support beneficial organisms; troubleshoot IPM failures and adapt strategies to novel pests or changing conditions. |
| 5 | Innovating | Conduct research on novel biocontrol agents, IPM tactics, or resistance mechanisms; develop site-specific pest risk models or decision support systems; publish guidance on emerging pest threats, invasive species, or resistance management. |
