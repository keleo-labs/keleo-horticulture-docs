# Plant Science Knowledge

[TOC]


Understanding of plant physiology, taxonomy, genetics, and ecology required to diagnose problems, select appropriate species/cultivars, and optimize growing conditions.

## Skill Levels

| Level | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | Recognize common plant families using visual characteristics; understand basic plant anatomy (roots, stems, leaves, flowers, seeds); awareness of photosynthesis, respiration, transpiration processes. |
| 2 | Applies | Identify plants using scientific nomenclature (genus, species, cultivar); diagnose common nutrient deficiencies and environmental stress symptoms; understand pollination biology and phenological stages. |
| 3 | Masters | Select cultivars based on climate adaptation, disease resistance, and performance targets; interpret complex plant responses to environmental manipulation (photoperiod, vernalization, stress); design propagation protocols for diverse species and methods. |
| 4 | Adapts | Develop novel cultivation strategies for emerging crops or extreme environments; integrate genetic knowledge to inform breeding or selection programs; solve complex diagnostic challenges (multiple stressors, rare diseases). |
| 5 | Innovating | Contribute to scientific understanding of plant-environment interactions through research; develop new cultivars through breeding, selection, or biotechnology; publish peer-reviewed research or lead industry workshops on plant science. |
